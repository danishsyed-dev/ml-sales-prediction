## Design Context

### Users
- **Target Audience**: Retail managers, business owners, and e-commerce analysts.
- **Context of Use**: Analyzing pricing strategies for products to optimize total revenue and forecast sales quantity.
- **Job to be Done**: Quickly input a product stock code, test a range of unit prices, and understand the price elasticity curve to set the optimal price.

### Brand Personality
- **Voice & Tone**: Sophisticated, clean, calm, and objective.
- **3-Word Personality**: Editorial, Minimalist, Precise.
- **Emotional Goals**: Clarity, focus, and confidence in data-driven decisions.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual Tone**: Warm, light monochrome base with a refined editorial magazine feel. Highly structured bento-style layout with elegant serif headers, muted sage/clay accent colors, and tabular numbers.
- **Theme**: Light/Dark responsive design with a smooth theme switcher.
- **Fonts**: Elegant serif headers (e.g., Playfair Display or Newsreader) paired with a clean, readable sans-serif body font (e.g., Satoshi, Outfit, or Geologica).
- **Colors**:
  - Warm Light Mode: Creamy off-white backgrounds, warm gray borders, charcoal text, and muted sage green (`#5f7d75` / oklch: `49.5% 0.035 155`) or soft clay accents.
  - Sophisticated Dark Mode: Warm off-black backgrounds (charcoal, not pure black), muted borders, off-white text, and soft sage accents.
- **Anti-References**: Avoid neon glows, cyan/purple gradients, generic drop-shadowed cards, and busy layout grids.

### Design Principles
1. **Typographic Contrast**: Use distinct sizing and serif headers to guide reading flow and make the interface look editorial.
2. **Whitespace is Luxury**: Double the padding and spacing where appropriate to let the layout breathe. Flatten card nesting.
3. **Tabular Data Precision**: Display all numbers, prices, and quantities in clear monospace/tabular-num formatting for fast scanability.
4. **Delightful Transitions**: Ensure any state changes (like theme switching, form validation, and predictions) animate smoothly with a natural deceleration curve.
